Traffic diagnostic

This free tool helps you find out why people are not buying. Are the wrong people visiting your page? Or is your page not doing a good job? Your answers save by themselves.

A "search word" is what someone typed before they found your shop. Etsy: go to Shop Manager > Stats > Traffic sources > keywords. Gumroad: go to Analytics > referrers. Other websites: look for a button called "Analytics" or "Traffic."

For each word, ask yourself: "Would someone who typed this really want to buy my exact product?" Match = yes. Mismatch = no, wrong people. Unclear = not sure.

Type how many people looked (views) and how many people bought (orders), for each place people find you. Find this in Etsy Stats > Traffic sources, or Gumroad Analytics.

Everything you type stays on your own device. It is never sent anywhere or seen by anyone else.
